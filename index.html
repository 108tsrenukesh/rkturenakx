Process

How we work

Seven stages — and you will always know which one your role is at.

  1. Requirement intake

    We take our time over the role: the skills, the team, the reporting line, the compensation band, and what has made it hard to fill so far.

  2. Sourcing and market mapping

    We begin with the people we already know, then source actively against the specific requirement.

  3. Screening conversation

    We speak with every candidate individually — understanding their suitability, confirming expectations, and making sure the interest is genuine before anything reaches you.

  4. Consent and shortlist

    We explain the role and the organisation to the candidate, and ask for their agreement before sharing their profile. You receive a curated shortlist, with our honest reservations set out alongside our recommendations.

  5. Interview coordination

    We schedule, confirm and follow up on every round, and carry feedback in both directions.

  6. Offer support

    We help with the offer conversation, keep expectations realistic on both sides, and work to make sure nothing comes as a surprise at the final stage.

  7. Documentation and joining

    We help the candidate gather whatever your onboarding needs, and stay in touch right through to the joining date.

Candidates

For candidates

Four things we would like you to know before you send us anything.

  1. We will never charge you a fee.

    Not to register, not on placement, not for anything at all. Our clients pay us. So if anyone using our name asks you for money, it is not us — please tell us, and we will help you deal with it.

  2. Your CV goes nowhere without your agreement.

    We will call you, tell you about the role and the organisation, and ask. Only then does your profile reach an employer.

  3. We look after your data, and delete it after 12 months.

    If we have not been in touch and neither have you, your CV comes off our records. You are welcome to ask us to remove it sooner, or to hold on to it for longer — just say.

  4. We cannot promise you a job — but we can promise honesty.

    No one honestly can. What we can offer is a straight answer about where you stand, including when the answer is no.

Questions

Frequently asked

How do you charge, and who pays?

Our clients pay us, and candidates are never charged at any stage, for any reason. Fees and commercial terms are agreed in writing with each client before we begin, with nothing left undisclosed.

Do you handle payroll for contract placements?

No — for contract and temporary roles the candidate joins your payroll or your payroll partner's, and we source, screen and coordinate. Salary, Provident Fund, ESI, professional tax, benefits and all statutory obligations stay entirely with the employer. We are not a staffing or payrolling company.

How quickly will we see profiles?

It depends on the role, the compensation band, and how deep the market is for that skill. We will give you a realistic view during the intake conversation rather than a number designed to win the mandate — and if we think a role will be difficult, we will say so early.

Do you conduct background verification?

Usually the employer runs verification, either directly or through their own agency. If you would like us to arrange it, we will engage a verification agency, let the candidate know before anything begins, obtain their explicit consent, and keep the process transparent to you throughout.

What happens to a candidate's data?

We hold it for 12 months from the last time we were in touch, and then delete it. We never share a profile with an employer before speaking to the candidate about that specific role and obtaining their agreement. There is fuller detail in our Privacy Policy.

What roles do you not recruit for?

Anything outside information technology. We also step away from mandates that ask us to screen or exclude candidates on grounds of gender, caste, religion, marital status, disability or age — whatever the value of the engagement.

Do you place candidates outside India?

Not at present. Our clients and candidates are in India, and our work is conducted entirely within India.

You are a new firm. Why should we trust you?

That is a fair question, and we are glad you asked it. The firm was established in 2026 and does not yet have a client list to show you. What it does have is seven years of individual experience behind it — at Wipro, DynPro, Marlabs, Annalect India and Hinduja Global Solutions — and a hundred-plus placements. We would rather share that openly than fill a website with numbers that are not ours.
