Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 August 2026

R K Turenakx Solutions, trading as RK Turenakx Talent Solutions ("we", "us", "our"), operates as a recruitment and talent acquisition services provider. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, how we use and protect it, how long we keep it, and the rights available to you.

We act as a Data Fiduciary under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025. Where you share personal data with us, you are a Data Principal under that Act.

Who this policy applies to

  • Candidates — individuals who share a CV or professional details with us, whether through our website, by email, or through any other channel
  • Clients — organisations engaging our recruitment services, and their representatives
  • Website visitors — anyone browsing this website

What we collect from candidates

Through our enquiry form, we collect: name; email address; telephone number; LinkedIn profile URL (optional); current industry; current role or designation; current annual compensation; expected annual compensation; notice period; preferred work locations; preferred industry; and your curriculum vitae, uploaded or emailed by you.

We do not ask you for, and you should not send us, government-issued identity documents such as Aadhaar, PAN, passport or driving licence at the enquiry stage. We do not require a photograph. We do not ask for your residential address.

About your CV. A CV is a document you author and control. It may contain information we have not asked for — date of birth, address, photograph, marital status, family details, or identity numbers. We do not require any of these and we do not use them in assessing your suitability for a role. You are free to remove them before sending your CV to us, and we encourage you to share only what you are comfortable sharing.

What we collect from clients

Business contact details of your representatives (name, designation, official email, telephone), and the role requirements, compensation ranges, and hiring process information you share with us for the purpose of the mandate.

What we collect from website visitors

Nothing. This website does not use analytics tools, advertising pixels, tracking scripts, or third-party profiling services of any kind. No cookies are set for tracking, analytics, or advertising. See the Cookie Notice.

Why we process your data, and on what basis

We process candidate personal data on the basis of your consent, given when you submit our form or send us your CV, for the following purposes and no others:

  • To assess your suitability against roles we are mandated to fill
  • To contact you regarding relevant opportunities
  • To discuss a specific role with you, including compensation, location and process
  • To share your profile with a prospective employer, after you have agreed to that specific role
  • To coordinate interview scheduling between you and the prospective employer
  • To assist you in assembling documents required for onboarding
  • To maintain records of our recruitment activity as required for our own operations

We process client data on the basis of the contractual relationship between us, for the purpose of delivering the recruitment services engaged.

We do not use your personal data for advertising, profiling, automated decision-making, or any purpose unconnected with recruitment. We do not sell your personal data. We do not share it for commercial gain.

Our consent-before-sharing commitment

We do not send your CV or profile to any employer without first speaking with you about that specific role and obtaining your agreement.

Our process is: we receive a mandate from a client; we identify potentially suitable candidates; we contact each candidate individually to explain the role, the organisation, the compensation and the process; we confirm your interest and your expectations; and only then do we share your profile with that client.

Your CV is never circulated speculatively, never sent to multiple employers at once without your knowledge, and never uploaded to job boards or shared with other recruitment agencies.

If you decline a role, we do not share your profile for it. Your decision has no effect on our willingness to consider you for other opportunities.

Who we share your data with

  • Prospective employers, on the consent basis described above, and only the specific employer you have agreed to
  • Background verification agencies, only in the specific circumstance described below
  • Service providers who host our systems, described below

We do not share your data with anyone else. We do not share it with other recruitment agencies, data brokers, marketing firms, or job aggregators.

Background verification

Background verification is ordinarily conducted by the employer, either directly or through a verification agency of their choosing. In that case we are not involved in the process and do not receive your verification data.

Where a client specifically requests that we arrange verification, we will inform you before any verification is initiated; tell you which agency will conduct it; obtain your separate, explicit consent before sharing your data with that agency; and share the process and its scope with the client so that it is transparent to all parties.

We will not initiate a background check on you without your knowledge.

Where your data is stored, and cross-border processing

Your data is held in email and cloud storage services provided by Google LLC (Gmail and Google Drive), used under standard terms as our business systems.

These are global services, and data held in them may be stored or processed on servers located outside India. We state this plainly because you are entitled to know it. Such transfers are permitted under Section 16 of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, which allows transfer of personal data outside India except to territories specifically restricted by the Central Government.

Our clients and our candidates are located in India. Our commercial activity is conducted entirely within India. We do not place candidates with employers outside India, and we do not represent candidates located outside India.

How long we keep your data

We retain candidate personal data, including CVs, for twelve (12) months from the date of your last interaction with us. At the end of that period, your data is deleted.

If you contact us during that period — by applying for a role, responding to us, or sending an updated CV — the twelve-month period restarts from that interaction.

You may ask us to delete your data at any time before the twelve months expire, and we will do so. You may equally ask us to retain it beyond twelve months if you would like to remain on our records, and we will honour that request.

Client and commercial records are retained for the period required under applicable tax and contractual law.

Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you have the right to:

  • Access — obtain a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how it is being processed
  • Correction — have inaccurate or incomplete data corrected, completed, or updated
  • Erasure — have your personal data deleted, subject only to any legal requirement on us to retain it
  • Withdraw consent — withdraw your consent at any time, as easily as it was given. Withdrawal does not affect processing carried out before withdrawal.
  • Grievance redressal — raise a complaint with us about how your data is handled, and receive a response
  • Nominate — nominate another individual to exercise these rights on your behalf in the event of your death or incapacity

To exercise any of these rights, write to rkturenakx@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Request". We will acknowledge within three (3) working days and respond substantively within thirty (30) days. There is no charge for exercising any of these rights.

How we protect your data

We apply reasonable security safeguards proportionate to the scale of our operations: access to candidate data is restricted to the proprietor alone; accounts holding personal data are protected by strong passwords and two-factor authentication; data is not stored on shared, public, or unsecured devices; data is not transmitted over unsecured channels; and data no longer required is deleted in accordance with the retention period above.

No system can be guaranteed absolutely secure. In the event of a personal data breach affecting your data, we will notify you and the Data Protection Board of India as required under the Act and Rules.

Children's data

Our services are directed at individuals seeking professional employment. We do not knowingly collect the personal data of any person under 18 years of age. If we learn that we hold such data, we will delete it. We do not process children's data for tracking, behavioural monitoring, or advertising.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date will change when we do. Material changes affecting how we use data already held will be notified to affected individuals by email.

Fraud & Fake Job Offer Advisory

Last updated: 19 August 2026

Recruitment is a frequent target of fraud in India. Please read this before responding to any communication that appears to come from us.

How we actually operate:

  • We contact candidates from rkturenakx@gmail.com and from +91 87926 28624
  • We never demand payment of any kind, at any stage
  • We never issue an offer letter ourselves — offer letters come from the employer
  • We never ask for your bank account details, UPI ID, card details, OTP, or passwords
  • We never ask you to install remote access, screen sharing, or trading applications
  • We never conduct recruitment exclusively over WhatsApp or Telegram without a verifiable email and telephone conversation
  • We never guarantee a job in exchange for payment

Warning signs of a fraudulent offer:

  • A demand for a registration fee, security deposit, or "refundable" amount
  • An offer letter received without any interview
  • Pressure to pay or decide within hours
  • Communication only from free email domains that misspell our name
  • Requests for Aadhaar, PAN, or bank details before any employer conversation
  • Compensation that is implausibly high for the role described
  • Grammatical errors and inconsistent company details in official-looking documents

If you suspect fraud using our name: stop communicating, do not pay, preserve the messages, and email the details to rkturenakx@gmail.com. We will confirm whether the communication is genuine and assist you in reporting it.

Fraud may also be reported to the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal at cybercrime.gov.in, or by calling the national cyber crime helpline on 1930.

R K Turenakx Solutions accepts no liability for losses arising from dealings with any person falsely representing themselves as associated with us. We will cooperate fully with any lawful investigation.

Code of Conduct & Ethical Recruitment

Last updated: 19 August 2026

These are the standards we hold ourselves to. They are commitments, not aspirations.

Towards candidates

  1. We never charge a fee.
  2. We never share your profile without your role-specific consent.
  3. We describe roles accurately, including compensation, location, work arrangement, and any known difficulty. We do not oversell a role to fill it.
  4. We tell you who the employer is before you agree to be submitted. We do not ask you to interview blind.
  5. We do not pressure you to accept an offer, resign from a role, or decline a competing offer.
  6. We keep your job search confidential. We do not contact your current employer, your references, or your colleagues without your permission.
  7. We tell you where you stand, including when the answer is no. Silence is not an acceptable outcome.
  8. We do not manipulate your CV. Any formatting change is cosmetic; we do not alter dates, titles, employers, or compensation figures.

Towards clients

  1. We present candidates honestly, including the reservations. We do not conceal known gaps to close a placement.
  2. We do not submit a candidate we have not personally spoken with.
  3. We do not submit the same candidate through multiple channels to inflate a fee.
  4. We hold your role requirements, compensation data, and hiring plans in confidence.
  5. We do not solicit candidates we have placed with you, and we do not use a client engagement as a source of candidates for another client.
  6. We decline mandates we are not equipped to deliver, rather than accept and underperform.
  7. We do not guarantee outcomes we cannot control.

Towards the law

  1. We comply with applicable labour, data protection, and commercial law.
  2. We do not deal in forged documents, fabricated experience, or falsified credentials. A candidate found to have submitted forged documents through us will not be represented by us again, and the client will be informed.
  3. We do not offer or accept bribes, kickbacks, or inducements.
